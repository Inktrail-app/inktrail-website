Account deletion

Permanent removal of your INKTRAIL account and all associated data
Delete INKTRAIL Account
Danger: Deleting your INKTRAIL account is permanent. This action cannot be undone.

How to request account deletion

  1. Sign in to your INKTRAIL account. You must be logged in to request deletion.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account.
  3. Click the "Delete account" button below and confirm.
If you do not have access to your account, contact support at support@inktrail.example for account recovery options before requesting deletion.

What will happen

  • All your personal data stored by INKTRAIL will be permanently deleted.
  • All projects, files, settings, and backups associated with your account will be removed.
  • Deletion is irreversible — you will not be able to recover the account or its data.
Note: You must be logged in to INKTRAIL to perform the deletion. Visit your account settings to access the Delete account option.
