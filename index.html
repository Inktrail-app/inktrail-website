TRUST & STANDARDS

The InkTrail Certified Standard

How we help travelers make informed, confident choices when discovering tattoo artists in new destinations.

VERIFIED REVIEW

InkTrail Certified Badge

The InkTrail Certified badge helps travelers identify artists and studios who have been personally reviewed by the InkTrail team.

Becoming InkTrail Certified requires a visit from the InkTrail team to verify your studio's hygiene standards, sterilization procedures, safety practices, and professionalism. Artists who meet our standards receive the official INKTRΛIL Certified badge on their profile.

01

Application & Studio Details

Artists apply through the portal, submitting studio credentials, health certifications, and verified portfolio artwork.

02

In-Person Verification Visit

The InkTrail team personally visits the studio to verify hygiene protocols, autoclave sterilization, needle disposal, and workstation cleanliness.

03

Certification Badge Awarded

Qualifying artists display the verified INKTRΛIL Certified badge on their profile, giving travelers instant peace of mind.

Important Notice: InkTrail does not verify licenses, certifications, or health standards unless explicitly stated. Certification indicates fulfillment of InkTrail internal review criteria and community trust indicators, but does not constitute a legal certification or medical guarantee.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About InkTrail

Find answers to help you navigate the platform as a traveler or tattoo artist.

What is InkTrail and how does it work?
InkTrail connects travelers with professional tattoo artists around the globe. You can search by destination or tattoo style, browse certified artists, view portfolios, and reach out directly to plan your session before or during your travels.
Is InkTrail free for travelers?
Yes! Searching for artists, browsing portfolios, and discovering tattoo styles across destinations is 100% free for all travelers.
How do tattoo artists join InkTrail?
Tattoo artists can create their profile by clicking "Create a Profile" and entering their details, portfolio, specialties, languages spoken, and contact methods.
What does the InkTrail Certified badge mean?
The InkTrail Certified badge is awarded after a personal review by the InkTrail team to verify a studio's hygiene standards, sterilization procedures, safety practices, and professionalism.
Where can I download the mobile application?
The InkTrail iOS application is available on the Apple App Store via our Download page. The Android version on Google Play is currently in development and will be available soon.
