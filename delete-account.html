INKTRΛIL Account Deletion

Permanent removal of your INKTRΛIL account, portfolio, and associated personal data.

Warning: Deleting your INKTRΛIL account is permanent and irreversible. All personal data, profile listings, and portfolio media will be permanently deleted.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. From within the Mobile App: Open the INKTRΛIL application, navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm your request.
  2. From the Web App: Sign in to your account at inktrailapp.com portal and initiate deletion from your account settings.
  3. Direct Support Request: If you no longer have access to your device, email our privacy team at inktrailapp@gmail.com from the email address registered with your account.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

  • Your personal profile, bio, certifications, and contact channels are removed immediately from public search.
  • All uploaded portfolio images and studio flash designs are permanently deleted from our storage.
  • Your active session tokens and login credentials are invalidated.
  • Account deletion cannot be undone. You will need to create a new profile if you wish to rejoin InkTrail.
