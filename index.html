INKTRΛIL OFFICIAL CERTIFIED BADGE

Personally Reviewed by InkTrail

The INKTRΛIL Certified badge identifies artists and studios that have undergone an in-person review by the InkTrail team.

Our review focuses on studio hygiene, cleaning and sterilization practices, workstation conditions, and professional standards. Artists and studios that meet our criteria earn the official INKTRΛIL Certified badge on their profile.

01

Application & Initial Review

Artists submit their profile details, studio information, and portfolio for an initial review.

02

In-Person Studio Visit

The InkTrail team visits the studio to assess hygiene practices, sterilization procedures, needle disposal, workstation cleanliness, and overall professionalism.

03

Certification Awarded

Artists and studios that meet InkTrail's review criteria receive the official INKTRΛIL Certified badge, helping travelers identify profiles that have undergone our review process.

Important Notice: INKTRΛIL certification reflects an internal review conducted by the InkTrail team at the time of the visit. It is not a government-issued license, legal accreditation, or medical guarantee, and it does not replace independent checks of local licensing requirements. Certification does not guarantee future conditions or eliminate all risks associated with tattooing.

THE STORY BEHIND INKTRAIL

Who’s Damien?

A backpacker on a mission to get a tattoo in every country I visit.

Hey, I’m Damien! I’m a backpacker with a personal mission: to get a tattoo in every country I visit, each one inspired by the place, its culture, or the people I meet along the way.

But as I traveled, I realized how difficult it could be to find the right tattoo artist in an unfamiliar destination. Searching through Instagram, relying on recommendations, dealing with language barriers, and trying to assess hygiene standards made finding an artist I could trust harder than it should be.

That’s where the idea for InkTrail came from.

I wanted to make it easier for travelers to discover local tattoo artists, explore their work, and connect with them directly. A way to discover authentic art wherever the road takes you, while helping talented artists share their work with an international audience.

InkTrail grew out of my own travel experiences, and I’m building it to help other travelers turn meaningful moments into tattoos they’ll carry for life.

“A travel tattoo is more than ink. It’s a reminder of a place, a culture, and the people you meet along the way.”

— Damien, Founder of InkTrail

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About InkTrail

Everything you need to know about discovering tattoo artists or joining InkTrail as an artist.

What is InkTrail and how does it work?
InkTrail helps travelers discover tattoo artists across Latin America. Search by destination or tattoo style, explore portfolios, find InkTrail Certified artists, and contact artists directly through Instagram or WhatsApp.
Is InkTrail free for travelers?
Yes! InkTrail is completely free for travelers. You can explore tattoo artists, browse portfolios, and discover styles without paying to use the platform.
How can tattoo artists join InkTrail?
Professional tattoo artists can create a profile through our artist portal. Add your portfolio, tattoo styles, studio information, and contact details to help travelers discover your work.
What does the InkTrail Certified badge mean?
The INKTRΛIL Certified badge identifies artists and studios that have undergone an in-person review by the InkTrail team. The review focuses on hygiene practices, sterilization procedures, workstation conditions, and professionalism. Certification reflects InkTrail's internal criteria and is not a government-issued license or a guarantee of risk-free tattooing.
Where can I download the mobile application?
Download InkTrail for iOS through the Apple App Store (and soon for Android on Google Play). Visit our Download page to access the app.
Does InkTrail handle tattoo bookings or payments?
Not currently. InkTrail helps travelers discover artists and contact them directly to discuss their tattoo projects. Booking arrangements and payments are handled directly between the traveler and the artist.
